# 🗓️ Weekly Schedule Planner

A sleek, privacy-first weekly planner built entirely with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. 

No databases, no servers. Your schedule and your data stay right in your own browser.

### ✨ What makes it cool?
* **100% Private:** Uses local storage so your data never leaves your device. 
* **Dynamic Layout:** Set your own start and end times, and the grid automatically builds itself to fit your day.
* **Smart Task Sizing:** Pick an end time and watch the task block stretch perfectly to fit your schedule.
* **Color-Coded Categories:** Quickly tag your tasks and filter them with one click.

### 🚀 How to get started
1. Clone this repo or download the files directly.
2. Open `index.html` in your favorite web browser.
3. Type in your preferred daily hours at the top and hit **Update Layout**.
4. Click on any empty slot to add a task, pick a category, and save!
